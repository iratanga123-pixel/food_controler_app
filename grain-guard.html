Smart Food Loss Prevention

Sensors in each store report temperature, relative humidity and grain moisture every hour. The model estimates how long the grain can stay safe, projects the trend 72 hours ahead, and alerts the storage manager by SMS before the grain spoils.

    How the system works

    In the store

    Sensor node

    Temperature and humidity sensor plus a capacitive moisture probe pushed into the grain, on a low-cost microcontroller with a battery or small solar panel.

    Connectivity

    Gateway

    Readings go out hourly over GSM, or over LoRa to a shared gateway where several cooperative stores sit close together.

    Cloud

    Prediction model

    Estimates safe storage days from crop, moisture, temperature and humidity, and projects the trend 72 hours ahead to warn early.

    People

    Alerts and dashboard

    SMS or USSD to the storage manager in Kinyarwanda or English, and this dashboard for the cooperative and extension officers.

    The scoring in this prototype is a rule-based stand-in for the trained model. It follows the common rule of thumb that safe storage time roughly halves for each 1% of moisture above the crop's safe level and for each 5–6 °C rise in temperature, with extra mould risk once air humidity passes about 70%. A production model would be trained on readings and loss records from real Rwandan stores.
